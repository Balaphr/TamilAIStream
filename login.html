FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tamil AI Stream really free to start?

Yes! Create an account and stream live FM radio plus unlimited Tamil songs with ads — completely free, no credit card required.

How does the AI music recommendation work?

Our AI learns from your listening history, favourite artists, songs you skip and re-play, then crafts a personalised mix — even generating playlists on command.

Can I listen to live Tamil FM stations?

Yes — live Tamil FM stations from Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Trichy, Kovai and more are always on air with real-time equalizer playback.

Does playback continue in the background?

Premium members enjoy background playback with the mini music player, so the music keeps playing while you use other apps or browse the web.

Can I listen on my phone, tablet and computer?

Absolutely. Tamil AI Stream is a responsive web experience that syncs your favourites and queue across all your devices.

Is my account and data secure?

Yes — accounts are protected with secure sign-in (including Google), and your library, favourites and preferences stay private to you.
